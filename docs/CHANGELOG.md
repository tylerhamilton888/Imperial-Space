# Changelog

## Scene asset kit • 22 November 2026

- Added four owner-selected visual targets, an editable four-view art project, three backdrops, twenty independent prop/character studies and reusable HUD/dialogue art.
- Captured the assembled studies at game resolution and checked imports, common wall anchors and Heavy pose switching.

## Developer Arena 0.1.2 • 22 November 2026

- Rebuilt the proving range as a civic arsenal forecourt with a staffed inspection point, pale paving, enamel-and-stone cover and a raised transit canopy.
- Registered the tall facade to the arena perimeter, added a solid inspection desk to navigation and placed the mixed-squad gunner beside the forward barricade. Character shadows now follow the actor's pose and sort beneath the fixtures.

## Art direction • 22 November 2026

- Added the owner-selected Karth Vale concept and gameplay capture criteria to the art guide. The current playable release is 0.1.1.

## Developer Arena 0.1.1 — 22 November 2026

- Replaced the Crusader, Khorgoth and range crew art with painted directional sprites. The Medium kit now reads through its high collar, ivory armor, brass edges, tapered waist and broad crimson cloak. Rifle, sword and defensive poses use registered foot pivots.
- Repainted the hall and its independently sorted fixtures. Crimson standards, warm work lamps, cooler paving and inlaid range markings separate the playable area from the walls and cover. The camera shows more of the north entrance.
- Replaced the title illustration and integrated a red-glass health gauge, illustrated weapon and medical slots, and brass-framed range controls. The HUD reflects spent rounds, health and medical charges.
- Adjusted mouse targeting and overhead health bars for the broader, taller Khorgoth silhouettes. Ground-plane collision, weapon damage and enemy timing retain the 0.1.0 rules.

## Developer Arena 0.1.0 — 22 November 2026

- Implemented menu, settings, controls, independent aim and movement, collisions, a persistent cloaked medium Crusader, a human scale reference, and an industrial training hall.
- Added shared health and hit queries, rifle, sword, dodge, guard/parry, reload, medical charges, feedback and defeat/retry. Raiders approach a last-seen target and gunners commit to a firing line.
- Added four seeded scenarios, active/passive spawning, cap and clearance checks, scale grid, refill, clear and a full one-action rebuild.
- Added original runtime visuals with editable sources, temporary synthesized sound, desktop export presets and focused Godot checks. See the [verification record](TEST_REPORT.md).

## Design handoff 1.0 — 22 November 2026

- Consolidated the Imperial Space creative direction into modular design and engineering specifications.
- Established fundamentals-first production gates and a permanent Developer Arena.
- Specified starter builds, proposed player and crew designs, and a functional opening operation with two outcomes.
- Included selected compressed art references with interpretation and provenance.
- Added asset validation and preview utilities plus separate context-maintenance templates.
- This release contains documentation and support utilities. It does not contain a verified game implementation.
