# Changelog

## Developer Arena 0.4.2 • 23 November 2026

- Registered a rigid cleaver to the Khorgoth’s painted fist in its walk, raised strike and recovery. The parry glint follows the weapon. The final blow holds the hit pose through the short impact pause.
- Added original artwork for the released cleaver, weapon forearm, shoulder armor and one-armed fall. Each loose piece tumbles and settles independently. A rifle finish drops the cleaver and plays the intact fall.

## Developer Arena 0.4.1 • 23 November 2026

- Redrew the Medium sword windup, contact and follow-through for lateral and diagonal attacks. The Crusader readies and carries the blade following sword use. Firing or reloading brings up the rifle.
- Replaced the Khorgoth raider's flattened standing silhouette with braced, kneeling, falling and grounded paintings. The shoulder lands with a low impact sound. Rifle kills leave the body intact and sword kills retain the detached weapon arm.
- Repainted both proving-ground fixture families. The folded shutter reveals its cable and inner rails. The broken barricade exposes its brace and leaves its sun-marked faceplate among the stones. Their shadows follow the changing shape.

## Developer Arena 0.4.0 • 23 November 2026

- The breach shutter dents, tears and opens. Both waist-high barricades crack, lose a section and collapse into traversable rubble. Rifle tracers, impacts, material sounds and debris originate on the struck fixture.
- A lethal sword hit splits the Khorgoth raider into a collapsing torso and a separately tumbling weapon arm. Rifle kills retain the whole body and carry through in the hit direction. Fallen parts bounce, stop at cover and clear with the encounter.

## Developer Arena 0.3.0 • 22 November 2026

- Added a sword riposte and rifle countershot after a timed frontal parry. A connected ordinary slash accepts a second press during recovery for a return cut.
- Added gunner line discipline, friendly obstruction of committed shots, and a playable Crossfire drill.
- Reworked the Medium actor's lateral steps, recoil, sword drive and hit feedback. Enemy bodies move against solids, retain a brief fallen silhouette, and reset with the encounter. Impact camera motion can be disabled in Settings.

## Developer Arena 0.2.0 • 22 November 2026

- Integrated the numbered range bays, practice targets, terminal and paired intact/breached masonry art in a playable proving ground.
- Added a three-stage drill with two defenders, objective feedback, physical jambs, and an opening that changes collision, gunfire and AI routing.
- Added title-screen entry, a fifth range preset and direct launch with `-- --breach`. F5 rebuilds the scene.

## Scene asset kit • 22 November 2026

- Added four owner-selected visual targets, an editable four-view art project, three backdrops, twenty independent prop/character studies and reusable HUD/dialogue art.
- Captured the assembled studies at game resolution and checked imports, common wall anchors and Heavy pose switching.

## Developer Arena 0.1.2 • 22 November 2026

- Rebuilt the proving range as a civic arsenal forecourt with a staffed inspection point, pale paving, enamel-and-stone cover and a raised transit canopy.
- Registered the tall facade to the arena perimeter, added a solid inspection desk to navigation and placed the mixed-squad gunner beside the forward barricade. Character shadows now follow the actor's pose and sort beneath the fixtures.

## Art direction • 22 November 2026

- Added the owner-selected Karth Vale concept and gameplay capture criteria to the art guide. The current playable release is 0.1.1.

## Developer Arena 0.1.1 — 22 November 2026

- Replaced the Crusader, Khorgoth and range crew art with painted directional sprites. The Medium kit now reads through its high collar, ivory armor, brass edges, tapered waist and broad crimson cloak. Rifle, sword and defensive poses use registered foot pivots.
- Repainted the hall and its independently sorted fixtures. Crimson standards, warm work lamps, cooler paving and inlaid range markings separate the playable area from the walls and cover. The camera shows more of the north entrance.
- Replaced the title illustration and integrated a red-glass health gauge, illustrated weapon and medical slots, and brass-framed range controls. The HUD reflects spent rounds, health and medical charges.
- Adjusted mouse targeting and overhead health bars for the broader, taller Khorgoth silhouettes. Ground-plane collision, weapon damage and enemy timing retain the 0.1.0 rules.

## Developer Arena 0.1.0 — 22 November 2026

- Implemented menu, settings, controls, independent aim and movement, collisions, a persistent cloaked medium Crusader, a human scale reference, and an industrial training hall.
- Added shared health and hit queries, rifle, sword, dodge, guard/parry, reload, medical charges, feedback and defeat/retry. Raiders approach a last-seen target and gunners commit to a firing line.
- Added four seeded scenarios, active/passive spawning, cap and clearance checks, scale grid, refill, clear and a full one-action rebuild.
- Added original runtime visuals with editable sources, temporary synthesized sound, desktop export presets and focused Godot checks. See the [verification record](TEST_REPORT.md).
